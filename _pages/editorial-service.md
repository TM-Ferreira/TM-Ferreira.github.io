Editorial work is an important part of my academic practice. I am particularly interested in constructive peer review, interdisciplinary exchange and the development of rigorous publication venues for natural hazards, structural resilience and the built environment.

## Current appointments

- **Founding Editor-in-Chief**, *GeoHazards* (MDPI), since 2020
- **Section Editor-in-Chief**, *Fire Risk Assessment and Safety Management in Buildings and Urban Spaces*, *Fire* (MDPI), since 2022
- **Associate Editor**, *Natural Hazards* (Springer Nature), since 2025
- **Associate Editor**, *Journal of Earthquake and Tsunami* (World Scientific), since 2026

I also contribute to topical and special collections covering multi-hazard risk, resilient urban areas, fire safety and geohazard characterisation.

## Reviewing and scientific assessment

I have reviewed for a broad range of international journals since 2012 and have served on research-assessment and funding panels in several countries. Current roles include membership of the EPSRC College of Reviewers and the British Council reviewer panel.

My peer-review contributions have been recognised through the 2026 *Natural Hazards* Editorial Contribution Award, an Outstanding Reviewer award from *Engineering Structures*, and a Publons Sentinel Award for peer review in engineering.
