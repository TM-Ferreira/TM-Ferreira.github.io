## Academic profile

Assistant Professor in Structural Mechanics and Structures at Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon. My work focuses on the structural vulnerability and resilience of historic buildings and urban areas exposed to natural and anthropogenic hazards, supported by digital tools for analysis, decision-making and community engagement.
