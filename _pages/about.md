I am an Assistant Professor in Structural Mechanics and Structures in the Department of Civil Engineering, Architecture and Environment at **Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon**.

My research focuses on the vulnerability and resilience of historic buildings and urban areas exposed to natural and anthropogenic hazards. I am particularly interested in earthquakes, fires, floods and landslides; the ways these hazards interact; and how physical, social and functional vulnerability can be considered together. A recurring aim of my work is to translate rigorous structural and risk analysis into useful evidence for communities, public authorities and practitioners.

## Research at a glance

- Structural vulnerability and seismic performance of masonry buildings
- Multi-hazard and multi-vulnerability assessment at building and urban scales
- Conservation and climate adaptation of built heritage
- GIS, data science, machine learning and immersive digital tools for risk-informed decisions
- Community engagement, risk communication and applied urban resilience

I have co-authored more than 250 scientific and technical publications, including over 100 articles in international journals, and contributed to 15 competitively funded research projects. My work has involved close collaboration with researchers, municipalities and professional organisations across Europe and beyond.

## Current roles

Alongside my appointment at Instituto Superior Técnico, I am a Visiting Professor at Yanshan University. I serve as the founding Editor-in-Chief of *GeoHazards*, Associate Editor of *Natural Hazards*, and Editorial Board Member/Handling Editor of the *Journal of Earthquake and Tsunami*. I am also a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy and a member of the EPSRC College of Reviewers.
