My teaching is grounded in structural mechanics, existing buildings, computational analysis and risk. I aim to connect fundamental engineering principles with field evidence, realistic case studies and the societal decisions that follow from technical assessment.

## Teaching experience

At Instituto Superior Técnico, my teaching and supervision are based in Structural Mechanics and Structures. Previous appointments at UWE Bristol and the University of Coimbra included responsibility for and teaching on:

- Computational Civil Engineering
- Engineering Graphics and Communication
- Surveying
- Foundation Mechanics
- Non-linear Structural Analysis
- Rehabilitation of Historic Structures
- Seismic Rehabilitation of Structures
- Masonry and Timber Structures

I have also taught or contributed to advanced courses at the University of Minho, the Federal University of Minas Gerais and Università G. d'Annunzio Chieti-Pescara.

## Approach

I use project-based learning, computational exercises, fieldwork and laboratory activities to help students connect analysis with real structures. Wherever possible, students work with authentic data and case studies involving existing buildings, historic centres and risk-reduction decisions.

## Supervision

As of May 2026, I had supervised or co-supervised:

- 1 postdoctoral researcher through a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowship
- 14 PhD researchers, including 9 completed doctorates
- 35 MSc students, including 30 completed dissertations
- 20 BSc students

I welcome enquiries from prospective researchers whose interests align with structural vulnerability, multi-hazard risk, built heritage, digital decision support or community resilience.
