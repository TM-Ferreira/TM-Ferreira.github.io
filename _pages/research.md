My research sits at the intersection of structural engineering, disaster risk reduction and built-heritage conservation. It moves between detailed investigation of individual masonry structures and large-scale analysis of neighbourhoods, historic centres and metropolitan areas.

## Research themes

### Structural vulnerability and seismic risk

I study the behaviour of existing masonry buildings, aggregates and structural components using field inspection, non-destructive testing, experimental evidence and computational analysis. This work supports vulnerability and fragility assessment, damage estimation and the evaluation of compatible strengthening strategies.

### Multi-hazard urban resilience

My research examines earthquakes, post-earthquake fire, urban fire, floods, landslides and climate-related hazards. I am especially interested in interactions between hazards and in frameworks that combine physical, social and functional dimensions of vulnerability.

### Digital tools and decision support

I use GIS, spatial analysis, machine learning, data fusion, BIM/HBIM and immersive environments to turn complex risk information into tools that can support collaborative decisions, emergency planning and public engagement.

### Built heritage and communities

Historic buildings and urban areas are both structurally distinctive and socially meaningful. My work connects technical assessment with conservation priorities, local knowledge, risk communication and community-led approaches to resilience.

## Current and recent projects

**ARTHUR - Advanced Resilience Techniques for Historical and Understudied Sacral Architecture (2026-2028)**  
Member of the research team in a Croatian Science Foundation project focused on the resilience of historic sacral architecture.

**RiHS-Clima - Systemic Risks in Human Settlements in a Changing Climate (2025-2027)**  
Local Coordinator for a CSIC-led project addressing systemic risks in settlements under changing climatic conditions.

**HRi-Cordis - Heritage at Risk and Community-driven Genius Loci Digitisation**  
Co-supervisor of a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship developing collaborative and immersive approaches to safeguard built heritage following hazardous events.

**Reerguer Leiria (2026)**  
Coordinator of an applied volunteer team supporting the assessment of storm-damaged homes following storm Kristin.

Earlier projects that I led include **MIT-RSCities**, on multi-risk interactions for resilient and sustainable cities; **VRisk**, on immersive BIM-enabled disaster scenarios; **VIS-Studio**, combining immersive reality and AI for collaborative decision-making; and **URBAN RISE**, on risk-informed data collection and education.

## Methods and collaboration

My work combines field surveys, laboratory and in-situ testing, structural modelling, probabilistic analysis, GIS, machine learning and participatory methods. I welcome collaboration with researchers, public authorities and practitioners working on structural resilience, heritage conservation and urban risk.
